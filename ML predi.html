WHAT THE ALERT MEANS

Rules used by this dashboard

−17 to −25 dBm: Good condition. Eligible areas may receive recognition marks.

Worse than −25 down to better than −30 dBm: 7-day preventive-action window.

−30 to better than −34 dBm: 5-day preventive-action window.

−34 to −38 dBm: 24-hour urgent-action window.

Below −38 but above −70 dBm: Immediate inspection required; range is more severe than the standard bands.

≤ −70 dBm: Full-loss alarm now. Multiple affected endpoints under the same area prefix raise a possible common fibre/PON/OLT-path alarm. Cable cut/theft is only a possibility and must be field-verified.
